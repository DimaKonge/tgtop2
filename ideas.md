# TG TOP Design Direction

TG TOP should feel like a **trusted, premium Telegram marketplace for verified communities**, not a game dashboard. The product uses compact mobile-first layouts, matte near-black surfaces, disciplined Telegram blue accents, and calm typography. The same group-card component is reused for featured placement and the general directory; only size and position change.

The primary product signals are verification, real audience data, clear ownership, and transparent placement. Avoid neon gradients, excessive blur, emojis as interface controls, gaming labels, visible rank numbers, or decorative empty-state noise. Empty placements use a restrained add-group action; real cards show Telegram-provided avatars, names, usernames, audience size, and an accessible path to detailed analytics.

Copy should be concise and confident, emphasizing verified communities, discovery, analytics, and transparent placements. The signature blue is reserved for primary actions, verification, and important navigation states.
