# SEO research notes — TG TOP public community pages

Public group pages must be server-rendered with stable canonical URLs and only expose data that the owner has explicitly enabled for search. A sitemap should list the canonical home URL and only the approved public community URLs. Private, anonymous, unlisted, or withdrawn groups must return 404 with `noindex` rather than leak data.
